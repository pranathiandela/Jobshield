About & Help

Built to make job searches safer.

JobShield is a college project focused on identifying suspicious job-posting patterns and helping applicants pause before sharing money or sensitive information.

What we check

Payment requests, unusual communication channels, unrealistic compensation, suspicious recruiter language, and other red flags.

What the score means

Low means fewer detected red flags, Medium means caution is recommended, and High means several strong warning signals were found.

Need help?

Start with Detector for a listing or Resume Screening for your CV. The project can later be connected to a trained ML model and external verification services.
