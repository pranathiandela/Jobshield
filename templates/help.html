About & Help

Understand how JobShield helps you search more safely.

Learn about JobShield AI and discover how its detection and screening features can help you evaluate job opportunities more carefully.

01 Who We Are & Our Purpose

JobShield AI is a college project focused on helping job seekers identify suspicious job opportunities before they share sensitive information, make payments, or proceed without verification.

Who we are

JobShield combines automated job analysis, suspicious-signal detection, and machine-learning assessment into one platform designed around safer job searching.

Our Mission

To help job seekers recognize potential warning signs and make safer, more informed career decisions.

Our Vision

To contribute to a more transparent hiring ecosystem where people can evaluate job opportunities with greater confidence.

02 Our Principles

What JobShield stands for.

Integrity

Present detection results clearly without pretending that automated analysis can replace independent verification.

Transparency

Show users the signals and information behind a detection result so they can understand why caution may be recommended.

Innovation

Combine practical rule-based checks with machine-learning techniques to strengthen automated screening.

User First

Keep the experience focused on helping users make better decisions during their job search.

03 What JobShield Provides

Multiple features working together to support safer job searching and better applications.

🔎 Job Detection

Paste a job description or upload a PDF, DOCX, or TXT job file. JobShield checks the available information for suspicious patterns and warning signals.

🤖 AI / ML Assessment

A trained machine-learning model provides an additional screening signal based on patterns learned from labelled job-posting data.

📊 Risk Analysis

Detection results include a risk score, risk level, suspicious signals, and suggested next steps.

📄 Resume Screening

Resume Screening identifies the likely professional domain, compares available skills with job requirements, and highlights relevant or missing skills.

🗂️ History & Dashboard

Completed checks can be reviewed through the application's Dashboard and History features.

📁 Job File Support

Detection supports text-based PDF, DOCX, and TXT job files, allowing users to analyze job postings without manually copying all of the text.

04 How JobShield Works

From a job posting to useful information for the user.

01 · Provide the Job

Enter the available job details and paste the description or upload a supported job file.

02 · Analyze

JobShield processes the available information using detection checks and a machine-learning signal.

03 · Review

Examine the risk level, score, suspicious signals, and AI/ML assessment.

04 · Verify

Independently verify the employer, recruiter, website, and opportunity before taking important action.

Important

Help / FAQ

Need help using JobShield?

Find quick answers about the project's main features.

01 How do I check a job?

Open Detector, enter the available job details, then either paste the job description or upload a supported PDF, DOCX, or TXT file and start the analysis.

02 What does the risk score mean?

The risk score summarizes the signals detected during analysis. A higher-risk result means more warning signals were identified and should be reviewed carefully.

03 What is the AI / ML assessment?

It is an additional machine-learning screening signal produced by a model trained on labelled job-posting data. It should be considered together with the rule-based findings.

04 Does a high-risk result prove that a job is fake?

No. Automated detection is a screening aid, not absolute proof. A suspicious result should encourage additional verification before you proceed.

05 Can I upload a job instead of pasting it?

Yes. Detection supports text-based PDF, DOCX, and TXT files. Files larger than the supported upload limit or files without readable text may not be accepted.

06 How does Resume Screening help?

Resume Screening evaluates the resume's likely domain and compares available skills with job requirements to provide useful matching and missing-skill feedback.
